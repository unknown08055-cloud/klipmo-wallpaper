# About Klipmo

Klipmo Wallpaper is a small, focused collection of wallpapers sized properly for two different screens: your phone and your laptop. No stretching a widescreen image to fit a portrait screen, and no cropping a phone shot into an awkward laptop background.

## What's here

- **Static wallpapers** — high-resolution images, downloaded as-is.
- **Live wallpapers** — short looping videos, for people who want their background to move. See the [live wallpaper guide](/guide/live-wallpapers/) for how to actually set one up on your device.

New wallpapers are added regularly, organized into categories so you can find something close to what you're picturing.

## A note on rights

Every wallpaper published here is either made for this site or used with permission. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, please get in touch through the [contact page](/contact/) and it will be reviewed and removed if appropriate.
