# Privacy policy

_Last updated: replace this date when you publish._

Klipmo Wallpaper ("we", "this site") publishes downloadable wallpapers. This page explains what data is collected when you visit.

## Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

Because this site serves ads, it participates in Google's advertising network, which may collect and use data as described in [How Google uses information from sites that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Cookies

Ads and basic site analytics (if enabled) may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

## What we don't do

We don't require an account to browse or download wallpapers, and we don't sell personal data.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the [contact page](/contact/).
